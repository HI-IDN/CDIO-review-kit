# Instructor Playbook

## What to delegate

Good candidates for automation:

- locating evidence;
- checking procedural requirements;
- computing Git metrics;
- identifying relevant commits/issues/PRs;
- finding inconsistencies;
- drafting feedback;
- preparing interview questions.

## What not to delegate completely

Instructor judgment is particularly important for:

- cognitive quality;
- borderline rubric decisions;
- individual contribution;
- interpretation of unusual collaboration patterns;
- suspected lack of understanding;
- consequential grading decisions.

## The handoff pattern

### Agent

Collect evidence and make a provisional assessment.

### Instructor

Check whether the evidence and interpretation make sense.

### Agent

Investigate specific uncertainties if requested.

### Instructor

Make the academic decision.

## Coverage before depth

During a time-constrained grading cycle, first obtain:

1. preliminary rubric coverage for every team;
2. major correctness issues;
3. draft feedback;
4. collaboration indicators;
5. questions requiring instructor attention.

Only then spend additional time on lower-priority coaching.
