# Assessment Rubric

Paste or convert the Canvas rubric here.

For each criterion preserve:

- criterion name;
- description;
- performance levels;
- points;
- weighting.

## Classification

Before grading, classify each criterion as:

- `PROCEDURAL`
- `COGNITIVE`
- `MIXED`

Procedural criteria may be reviewed repo-by-repo.

Cognitive criteria should normally be calibrated across submissions before
final scores are proposed.

Do not change rubric requirements during assessment.
